The nonlinear speed slider reserves substantial travel for 0–20 rpm. The 720° cycle slider and step buttons provide deterministic inspection of every kinematic state.

Valve guides and spring seats remain fixed while valves, retainers and buckets move; inner and outer springs compress with measured lift.

External fittings, plugs and electrical lugs are mounted to explicit component surfaces rather than placed as unsupported decoration.

The engine is not imported CAD. Every visible part is generated at runtime from parametric primitives and curves in docs/engine/.
